# AI + 3D Science Lesson Review

**Status:** Completed and public  
**Role:** Learning experience designer, content designer, assessment designer, and product owner  
**Format:** Ten-minute interactive microlearning course

## Problem

Educators may use generative AI to review lessons, but fluent responses can overstate alignment, invent lesson features, or encourage users to surrender professional judgment. Educators need a short, practical workflow that combines three-dimensional science criteria with responsible AI use.

## Solution

The course teaches educators to:

- Recognize integrated Science and Engineering Practices, Disciplinary Core Ideas, and Crosscutting Concepts
- Write criteria-based prompts that require traceable lesson evidence
- Identify confident but unsupported AI claims
- Protect student information
- Check for scientific accuracy, accessibility, bias, and missing evidence
- Retain human responsibility for the final instructional decision

## Learning architecture

- Three measurable objectives
- Concise explanation of three-dimensional learning
- Interactive knowledge checks with immediate feedback
- Branching decision scenario
- Five-question final assessment
- Completion statement
- Downloadable one-page job aid
- Learner-feedback survey

## Accessibility decisions

The course uses semantic headings, keyboard-operable interactions, labeled form controls, visible focus behavior, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, high-contrast colors, plain language, and a printable job aid.

## Evaluation

Functional checks cover navigation, answer feedback, scoring, download behavior, mobile layout, keyboard access, and completion flow. The next evaluation stage is a small educator pilot measuring usefulness, clarity, confidence, and intended behavior change.
