# Growth Over Wealth

**Status:** Functional prototype in formal audit  
**Role:** Product owner, learning-system designer, curriculum architect, and testing decision-maker  
**Audience:** Students, teachers, school leaders, and implementation teams

## Problem

Traditional behavior systems often record incidents or distribute rewards without creating a coherent pathway for reflection, repair, skill development, meaningful responsibility, and sustained growth.

## Product direction

Growth Over Wealth is a connected learning and behavior ecosystem designed to make growth, responsibility, reflection, financial decision-making, recognition, and student agency visible.

The product architecture includes student, teacher, and administrator experiences; role-based permissions; learning content; recognition systems; reflection paths; virtual-economy rules; reporting; and protected operational workflows.

## My responsibilities

- Identified the classroom and implementation problem
- Defined the product vision, language, user roles, and core journeys
- Translated educator needs into system rules and acceptance criteria
- Designed curriculum, student experiences, and intervention pathways
- Used AI-assisted workflows for ideation, organization, prototyping, and quality review
- Reviewed test findings and made release-scope decisions
- Maintained separation between incentives, reflection, intervention, and confidential records

## Product principles

1. Growth is more important than point accumulation.
2. Students need meaningful ways to repair harm and practice better decisions.
3. Schoolwide systems require clear staff expectations and usable workflows from day one.
4. Permissions, privacy, accessibility, and recovery must be verified before broad use.
5. A polished interface is not evidence that a product is ready for release.

## Testing approach

The audit includes role-based login testing, authorization checks, data consistency, workflow recovery, reduced-motion behavior, accessibility checks, build verification, staged deployment, defect documentation, and explicit release blockers.

## Current limitation

The prototype remains in formal audit. This repository intentionally does not claim full production readiness and does not expose private source code, credentials, student data, database details, or sensitive audit records.
