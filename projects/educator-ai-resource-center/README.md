# Educator AI Resource Center

**Status:** Completed and public  
**Role:** Content strategist, knowledge-system designer, learning product designer, and product owner  
**Format:** Searchable educator knowledge base and content-governance demonstration

## Problem

AI guidance can quickly become scattered, duplicated, difficult to find, inconsistently reviewed, or outdated. Creating more content does not solve the problem unless the organization also creates a sustainable system for ownership, findability, quality, review, and retirement.

## Solution

The Educator AI Resource Center demonstrates a governed content ecosystem with:

- Searchable help articles
- Content categories and tags
- Named owners and intended audiences
- Version numbers and lifecycle statuses
- Scheduled review dates
- Approval records
- Reusable intake, authoring, QA, and retirement templates
- Accessibility standards
- Archive and replacement rules
- Decision rights and review cadence

## Taxonomy

The demonstration organizes content across Responsible AI, Prompting, Science Review, Accessibility, and Implementation. Tags support cross-category discovery for privacy, assessment, workflow, accessibility, safety, design, facilitation, and quality.

## Governance model

The lifecycle moves through request, draft, subject-matter review, accessibility and metadata QA, approval, publication, scheduled review, revision, replacement, or retirement. High-risk and policy-related content receives additional approval.

## Testing

Functional checks cover keyword search, category filters, status filters, tag filters, article metadata, modal interaction, navigation, responsive layout, and template downloads.
